Why Neoprene for Your Cybertruck?

Neoprene — the same material used in wetsuits — offers excellent water resistance, UV protection, and a comfortable feel year-round. It's ideal for Cybertruck owners who haul gear, take trail trips, or simply want rugged daily protection.
